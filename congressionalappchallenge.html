Know your zone

Find your evacuation zone before a storm is near. Sarasota County uses zones A through E. A floods first and leaves first. E is safest. Look up your address on the county map at scgov.net. If your zone is ordered to evacuate, leave early. Don't go to a shelter until the county says it's open. If someone at home has medical needs, sign up for the county's Medical Needs Program ahead of time.

When a storm is coming
During the storm

Stay inside, away from windows. If it goes calm, that's the eye. Don't go out. The other side is still coming. If water comes in, go up. Never walk or drive through floodwater.

After it passes

Stay out until it's safe. Power lines can still be live, and water can be dirty or dangerous. If you left, wait for the all-clear before going home. Check on neighbors once it's safe.

Privacy

Sarasota Stormlines is a student project for the Congressional App Challenge.

What you give the app

An address: street, city, state, and ZIP. That's it.

How it's used

Your address is used to look up risk info for that spot. It's sent to public government data services to find your coordinates, elevation, evacuation zone, nearby water, tides, and any active storm: the U.S. Census Bureau (address lookup and water data), the U.S. Geological Survey (elevation), NOAA and the National Hurricane Center (tides, storms, surge), and Sarasota County (evacuation zones).

What's saved

The app saves your last address in your own browser, on your device, so you don't have to retype it. It stays on your device, is never sent to a server, and the developer never sees it. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

What's not collected

No accounts. No database. No cookies. No tracking or analytics. The app does not keep your address or any personal information on a server.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared beyond the government services above, and nothing is used for advertising.

Changes

This policy may change as the app develops.
